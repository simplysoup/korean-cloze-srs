# Bunpro Decks + Grammar Library study — 2026-11-09

Live study on bunpro.jp as simplysoup (Lv. 30), desktop 1919px. Nothing
submitted, completed, started, added, or removed. View toggles and
collapses tried were restored (Grammar Library left expanded Grid view,
Decks left on Explore).

## Decks page (https://bunpro.jp/decks; /decks/explore is the catalogue)

- A grid of cards, 5 per row at desktop, inside a centred column about
  1,400px wide. Rich/illustrated, not utilitarian. Page background white;
  header dark teal/navy. Accent coral-red (Official Deck, Advanced Search,
  View All, bookmark icons, Learn/Review count pills, progress bar).
- Top: a dark-teal hero band with a Featured Deck, then two overlapping
  pill-tabs straddling the hero/white boundary (My Decks grey inactive,
  Explore white active), then a centred search box + red Advanced Search.
- Browse by Type: two rows of 5 large rounded tiles (~270x80px, ~20px
  radius, glossy gradient with a large faded Japanese word watermark and
  a small white label top-left): Grammar, Vocab, Textbook, Anime, Manga,
  Game, TV/Movie, Novel, Music, Misc.
- 10 sections in fixed type order (Grammar, Vocab, Textbook, Anime,
  Manga, Game, TV/Movie, Novel, Music, Misc), 47 cards total. Each
  section header: bold dark heading at left, an outlined red "→ View
  All" pill next to it, a thin light-grey rule across the rest of the
  row. Within Grammar and Vocab the order is N5 → N4 → N3 → N2 → N1.
  Sections separated by ~40–60px vertical whitespace.
- Each deck card (~270px wide x 330px tall, rounded ~16px, thin
  light-grey border, very soft/no shadow):
  - Top ~55% is a full-bleed illustrated cover image (the title artwork
    itself). No separate title text over the cover beyond the artwork.
  - A white circular bookmark button (red outline bookmark icon) floats
    at the bottom-right corner of the cover, overlapping the cover/body
    boundary.
  - Bottom ~45% is a flat pale-grey panel (no shadow, straight edge).
    Top to bottom: deck title bold dark (~15px); underneath, small red
    text with a Bunpro logo mark "Official Deck" (or "Created by
    <username>" in grey); then three statistics in a row separated by
    thin vertical grey dividers — Grammar count (bold number, "Grammar"
    beneath), Vocab count, Learners ("10k+", "5k+", "100+", "<10").
  - Bottom-centre: small muted-grey "Details" text-link.
  - The whole card is a link; NO Study, Add, or Learn button on Explore
    cards, and no description/subtitle beyond the title and creator line.
- Progress: NO printed percentage or learned/total number on the card,
  and no conventional progress bar in the panel. Only a thin (~3–4px)
  red/coral line along the very bottom edge of the cover image, width
  proportional to the Learned percentage (e.g. N5 Grammar 132/132 = 100%
  full width; N4 Grammar 13/185 = 7% a short stub at the left; 0% no
  line). At 100% it reads as a full-width dark burgundy/red strip.
  Community decks show no progress line.
- Hover: a ~2px solid red outline around the whole card; no other change.
- My Decks tab: same card design, three sub-sections — Learn Queue
  Decks (a small white pill with a red book icon and a count at the
  bottom-left of the cover = reviews/Learn items due), Custom Decks,
  Bookmarked Decks.

## Grammar Library (https://bunpro.jp/grammar_points)

- Correction: the default view is NOT a list of rows. Points are small
  cards in a 4-column grid. Even the "List" toggle does not produce rows
  (it is a level/lesson picker: level buttons, then lesson-number
  buttons, then one lesson container with the same 4-column card grid).
- Page chrome: white background, no page title heading. Toolbar: a wide
  search box at left (~40% width), three equal white buttons with thin
  grey borders and ~8px radius (Select Items, Hide Learned Items, Add
  Filters), and a small "Grid" toggle at the far right. Content column
  spans almost the full viewport width, ~24px side margins.
- Level sections (N5, N4, N3, N2, N1, Non-JLPT): each level is ONE large
  containing white card — thin light-grey border, rounded ~12px, no heavy
  shadow, 24–32px vertical gap between levels. This shared container is
  the main "connected" device: everything in the level sits inside it.
  - Level header: a full-width strip at the top of the container, very
    light grey background (near #F5F5F5) with a thin light-grey divider
    beneath it, ~60px tall. Left to right: a small dark chevron, the
    level name in large bold dark type (~22–24px), and at the far right
    a small diagonal expand-arrows icon. A small red Bunpro mark appears
    before the level name when the level is fully learned. No item count
    or percentage in the header.
  - Inside the container, lesson groups sit on the same white background
    — NOT indented relative to the level header, no left border or
    connecting line. The container's own padding (~24px sides) is the
    only inset. Belonging comes from the shared outer card + the grey
    header strip + horizontal divider rules, not indentation or a spine.
- Lesson groups: N5–N1 each have 10 lessons; Non-JLPT has 2.
  - Lesson header: a row ~45px tall on white, with a thin light-grey
    horizontal divider ABOVE it (separating it from the previous
    lesson's cards) — the divider is the attachment device. Left to
    right: a small dark chevron, a small red Bunpro mark when the lesson
    is fully learned, then "Lesson 1" in bold dark type (~16px), an
    en-dash, and the lesson subtitle in regular-weight muted dark grey
    on the SAME line. The lesson NUMBER is distinguished only by bold
    weight (and the word "Lesson"); there is no circle, badge, coloured
    chip, or larger size for the number itself. N1 lessons have no
    subtitle (bare "Lesson 1", "Lesson 2", …).
  - Point cards are directly under their lesson header, in the 4-column
    grid, with no extra divider between header and grid.
- Point cards: pale-grey (#F2F2F2-ish) rectangles, rounded ~8px, no
  visible border or shadow, ~1/4 of the container width each (~430px at
  this viewport), ~90–110px tall, ~16px gaps both ways. Inside (top to
  bottom, left-aligned, ~12–16px padding):
  1. The form in bold dark type (~17–18px); where the form has kanji,
     its reading follows on the same line in smaller light-grey type.
  2. The English meaning in regular muted grey (~14–15px), clamped to
     one line with an ellipsis.
  3. Two tiny pills at bottom-left: a light-grey pill with the level
     ("N5"), and, only for learned items, a dusty-rose/pale-pink pill
     with a tiny Bunpro mark and the SRS stage ("Expert 1",
     "Seasoned 3", "Adept 1", "Beginner 1"). Unlearned items show only
     the level pill.
  4. A vertical three-dot menu button, dark grey, centred vertically at
     the card's right edge — the only per-point control; no Study/Add
     button on a card.
- Collapsed vs expanded: clicking a lesson header flips its chevron
  ("⌄" to ">") and the whole card grid vanishes — only the header row
  remains, between its divider lines; no count, summary, or preview.
  Clicking a level header collapses the entire level to just its ~60px
  light-grey header strip, with no lessons, cards, counts, or progress.
  Re-expanding restores everything exactly.
- Summary of the "connected" feeling: level = one big white bordered
  card + grey header strip + divider rules; lessons = no indentation,
  no spine, plain rows separated only by full-width hairline dividers;
  the lesson number is bold-inline, not a badge; points = flat
  pale-grey cards with a grey English line and small grey/rose pills.
  Hierarchy: level name largest/boldest, lesson header next (bold
  "Lesson N" + regular subtitle), point form bold, meaning grey, pills
  smallest and muted. Colour is restrained — white, light greys, dark
  text, red used sparingly — versus the Decks page, which is far more
  colourful and image-led.

## Limits

Colours are described from screenshots, not measured hex values; card
dimensions are approximations at 1919px viewport. No deck detail page,
lesson, quiz, or review was opened, and no point card menu, bookmark,
or Details control was clicked.
